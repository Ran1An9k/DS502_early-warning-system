# Intervention Workflow

## Purpose

The intervention workflow describes what happens after the model identifies a student as academically at risk.

## Workflow

1. **Predict**
   - The model predicts whether the student is At Risk or Not At Risk.

2. **Explain**
   - The system provides the main factors that contributed to the prediction.

3. **Advisor Review**
   - The Academic Advisor reviews the prediction and the student's academic information.
   - The model prediction is used as decision support and does not automatically trigger academic penalties.

4. **Intervention**
   - If the advisor confirms that support is needed, an appropriate intervention is selected.
   - Possible interventions may include:
     - Academic advising meeting
     - Study planning support
     - Tutoring or academic support services
     - Attendance follow-up
     - Referral to relevant student support services

5. **Follow-up**
   - The advisor follows up with the student after the intervention.
   - The outcome of the intervention is recorded.

6. **Evaluate**
   - The student's academic progress is reviewed in a later period.
   - The intervention outcome can be used to evaluate whether the support was effective.

## Important Note

The model is an early-warning support tool. Final decisions should remain with the Academic Advisor and should not be made automatically by the model.
